Something else on your mind?
Just ask Josh ↗
Do you come to my home?+
Josh provides mobile mechanic service in North Broward County by appointment. Share your city and work location so he can confirm coverage, access, and a safe place to work.
Is my selected date a confirmed appointment?+
Not yet. The date and time window you select are preferences. Josh confirms availability, the work, location, parts, and price directly with you before an appointment is booked.
Can I send photos or a video for a quote?+
Yes. Select photos or a short video in the request form. On supported phones you can share the files and request through your sharing menu. Otherwise, open the prepared WhatsApp message and attach your files in the chat. This website does not silently upload your files.
Can I bring my vehicle to Josh instead?+
Ask Josh about an agreed meeting location when requesting service. There is no public walk-in address; all arrangements are by appointment only.
How much will the work cost?+
Pricing depends on your vehicle, the job, the parts needed, and access. Send the year, make, model, symptoms, and photos. Josh will discuss an estimate before you approve any work.
Do I need an account to request service?+
No. The public website and request form do not require a login. You finish sending the request in WhatsApp, text, or your email app. Only the private owner workspace needs authentication.
What should I include in my request?+
Include your name, phone number, vehicle year, make and model, the service or symptoms, your city, and a preferred date. Photos of the affected area or a brief video can help. Please avoid including identification documents, payment details, or people without permission.
