Privacy & service requests

Effective October 25, 2026.

What the public form does

The request form collects your contact details, vehicle make and model, VIN, preferred date, work description, and service address. You may submit online to Josh’s private booking workspace or choose to send a prepared message using WhatsApp, SMS or email. Your selected date is a request, not a confirmed appointment.

Your photos and videos

Selected files and their previews remain on your device while you complete the form. The website does not upload them in the background. If you click Submit Online, supported attachments of up to 2.5 MB combined are saved to Josh’s private booking workspace with the request. You can instead use your phone’s share menu or attach media in WhatsApp, text or email. Those providers handle the information according to their own policies. Please send only relevant vehicle photos; avoid identity documents, payment details, precise home address images, and other people’s information.

What Josh receives

When you send your request, Josh receives the details and files you send through that channel and uses them to discuss the job, prepare an estimate, arrange the appointment, and contact you about the service. The service address and VIN are stored in Josh’s private workspace when you submit online, so he can plan the visit and verify compatible parts.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Hosting infrastructure may process routine technical information such as an IP address, requested page, browser information, and security logs. We have not installed advertising trackers, session-replay tools, or marketing analytics on this version of the site.

Private workspace

Only approved staff can view stored requests, VINs, photos, appointment details, private job notes and manually logged conversations. Staff access uses a secure server-side session. We do not automatically capture customer SMS messages, send automated reminders, or synchronize to Google Drive or Calendar in this version. Contact Josh to request correction or deletion.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact joshmobileinc@gmail.com or (646) 796-7322 about your request, corrections, or deletion of information you have sent. Do not use this website for emergency roadside assistance.
