Privacy & service requests

Effective October 25, 2026.

What the public form does

The request form prepares a message containing the details you enter: your contact information, vehicle, work requested, city, and preferred date. It does not automatically send that message or create a confirmed booking. You choose a messaging or email app and send it to Josh.

Your photos and videos

Selected files and their previews remain on your device while you complete the form. The website does not upload them in the background. Files are shared only when you use your phone’s share menu or attach them in WhatsApp, text, or email. Those providers handle the information according to their own policies. Please send only relevant vehicle photos; avoid identity documents, payment details, precise home address images, and other people’s information.

What Josh receives

When you send your request, Josh receives the details and files you send through that channel and uses them to discuss the job, prepare an estimate, arrange the appointment, and contact you about the service. An exact service address can be agreed privately after contact.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Hosting infrastructure may process routine technical information such as an IP address, requested page, browser information, and security logs. We have not installed advertising trackers, session-replay tools, or marketing analytics on this version of the site.

Private workspace

Google-backed storage, owner authentication, job records, and reminders require a separate account authorization. They are not represented as active until that setup is completed. The public dashboard preview uses clearly labeled fictional sample records only.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact joshmobileinc@gmail.com or (646) 796-7322 about your request, corrections, or deletion of information you have sent. Do not use this website for emergency roadside assistance.
