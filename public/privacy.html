YOUR INFORMATION. HANDLED WITH CARE.

Privacy & service requests

Effective October 25, 2026.

Information you choose to provide

Our service form collects your name, phone, optional email, vehicle year/make/model and VIN, work requested, service address, city, and preferred appointment date. You may add photos or videos of the vehicle. Please do not include identity documents, payment details, or unrelated personal information.

Submitting a request

Reviewing the form does not send it. Selecting “Submit service request” saves one private request and uploads selected media to private storage used by Josh Mobile. Josh and approved administrators can access these records to assess the work, prepare parts, arrange appointments, and communicate about the service. A request is not a confirmed appointment until Josh confirms it with you.

Texting, WhatsApp and email

After a request is saved, you may open a prepared message in your preferred app and send it to Josh yourself. Selected files are not automatically attached to messaging links. Messaging providers process information under their own policies. Your phone's message history is separate from this website; staff can record relevant conversation notes in the private workspace. The website does not automatically import text or WhatsApp messages.

Optional Telegram owner notifications

If Josh chooses to enable owner Telegram alerts, the private server may send the designated owner-only Telegram chat a new service-request summary, including the reference, customer name, phone, VIN, year/make/model, requested service, service address and location type, problem description, preferred appointment time, preferred contact method, and counts of selected photos and videos. Telegram processes that message according to its own privacy terms. No customer photo/video files or public links to private attachments are sent to Telegram. This feature is disabled until Josh explicitly configures it; a failure to notify does not cancel a saved service request.

Private storage and staff access

The website and private job storage use Vercel. Customer records and media are not published on the public website or in the source-code repository. Approved staff use individual accounts, and sign-in uses a secure session cookie. Customers do not need an account. We retain job records to manage service and follow-up; contact Josh to request correction or deletion of your information, subject to applicable recordkeeping needs.

Security and technical information

Hosting providers process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for site delivery, security, and troubleshooting. We use limited security counters to reduce automated abuse. No advertising analytics or payment collection is included in this site. No online system can guarantee absolute security; please send only information needed for your service request.
